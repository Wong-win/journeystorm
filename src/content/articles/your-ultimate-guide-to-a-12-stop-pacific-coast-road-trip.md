Later, explore the unique McCloskey Castle, perched atop a lofty hill with commanding ocean views. Over the years, the castle has served various purposes, from an underground abortion clinic to a vibrant party palace. Today, it's an eccentric venue showcasing an intriguing mix of furnishings and decor. A tour of this castle is a must during your Pacific Coast road trip.</p>
